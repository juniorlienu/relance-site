About Relance

Built so a busy installer never loses a lead to a missed call.

Big firms have a receptionist. Independent installers have a ladder, a van, and a phone that rings at the worst possible moment. Relance levels that field.

What we do

We catch the calls you can’t

Relance is a lead-recovery system for independent boiler and heat pump installers. The moment a call goes unanswered, it texts the customer back in seconds, answers their real questions from your genuine prices, holds the conversation like a member of your team, and drops the lead straight into your pipeline — day or night, evenings, weekends and days off. You change nothing about how you work; it simply stops leads leaking while your hands are full.

Why we do it

Because the best installer doesn’t always win the job — the one who answers does

A missed call isn’t a missed chat. It’s a boiler replacement, a heat pump install, a four to five-figure job quietly dialling the next number down the list. Skilled, trustworthy tradespeople lose that work every week for one reason: they were up a ladder when it rang. That never sat right with us. Relance exists so the quality of your work — not the timing of a phone call — decides who gets the job.

Our promises

What you can hold us to

  • ✓We never invent prices or appointments. Every reply comes from your real business facts. Prices are given as ranges, firm figures need a site visit, and it never makes up a slot.
  • ✓It sounds like your firm, not a call centre. Knowledgeable, local, to the point — the way a good tradesperson texts a customer back.
  • ✓It knows its limits. When a lead needs a human, it hands them to you rather than guessing — and flags it so nothing slips.
  • ✓We recover leads the right way. It replies to people who contacted you first, includes an opt-out, and is set up in line with UK PECR rules. No spam.
  • ✓No long tie-in. Cancel any time. We’d rather earn your place each month than trap you in a contract.
  • ✓You stay in control. When you pick up, nothing happens. Relance is a safety net, never a replacement for you.
