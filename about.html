About Relance

Built so high-value renewables installers never lose customers to unanswered enquiries.

The switch to clean energy doesn’t happen all at once. It happens one home at a time. Relance was built to recover the enquiries renewables firms miss, so more of those homes get booked in sooner.

The person behind it

Why I chose this industry, and not another one

As far as I can remember, I’ve always held a strong connection to nature. When I was younger I would get upset in class hearing what pollution was doing to our planet, our home. I’d ask myself why nobody seemed to be doing anything about damage everyone agreed was irreversible. That was my own ignorance. I assumed there was nothing I could do.

That changed when I understood where the bottleneck actually sits. The world doesn’t lack people who want renewable, cost-efficient sources of energy running their homes. It lacks installers with a spare hand to answer them. Every unanswered call is a home putting out more carbon than it needs to.

It almost seems contradictory to use AI for this, because running these models isn’t free, environmentally speaking. I can’t pretend otherwise. But the choice is between an AI conversation and no conversation- which is what most of these enquiries are met with. And this could be the difference between a home becoming renewable or not. I prefer to leverage AI to put that work in front of the people who can actually do it.

Relance can’t fix the climate by itself. No software can. It takes installers who can do the work, and a way for that work to find them. The second part is mine.

Junior Lienu, founder

What we do

We recover the enquiries you never knew you were losing

Relance is a lead recovery system for UK renewables installers: heat pump and boiler firms, and solar and battery firms. Trades where a single enquiry is worth thousands, and where the people who do the work are the people who answer the phone. The moment a call goes unanswered, it texts the customer back in seconds, holds a real conversation, establishes what predicts a sale, and books the survey into your diary with the address and what it learned attached. Day or night, evenings, weekends and days off. It never puts a price on a job nobody has seen. You change nothing about how you work; it simply stops leads leaking while your hands are full.

Why we do it

Because the best business doesn’t always win the customer. The one who answers does.

A missed enquiry isn’t a missed chat. It’s an eleven thousand pound heat pump, a solar and battery install, a survey worth more than most firms take in a fortnight, quietly dialling the next number down the list. Skilled, trustworthy installers lose that work every week for one reason: they were up a ladder when it rang. That never sat right with us. Relance exists so the quality of your work, not the timing of a phone call, decides who gets the job.

Our promises

What you can hold us to

  • ✓We never invent prices or appointments. Every reply comes from your real business facts. Prices are given as ranges, firm figures need a site visit, and it never makes up a slot.
  • ✓It sounds like your business, not a call centre. Knowledgeable, local and to the point, the way you would text a customer back on a good day.
  • ✓It knows its limits. When a lead needs a human, it hands them to you rather than guessing, and flags it so nothing slips.

  • ✓You stay in control. When you pick up, nothing happens. Relance is a safety net, never a replacement for you.
