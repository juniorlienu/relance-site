About Relance

Built so busy businesses never lose customers to unanswered enquiries.

Big companies have someone to answer the phone. Independent businesses have both hands full and a phone that rings at the worst possible moment. Relance levels that field.

What we do

We recover the revenue you never knew you were losing

Relance is a revenue recovery system for UK renewables installers: heat pump and boiler firms, and solar and battery firms. Trades where a single enquiry is worth thousands, and where the people who do the work are the people who answer the phone. The moment a call goes unanswered, it texts the customer back in seconds, holds a real conversation, establishes what predicts a sale, and books the survey into your diary with the address and what it learned attached. Day or night, evenings, weekends and days off. It never puts a price on a job nobody has seen. You change nothing about how you work; it simply stops leads leaking while your hands are full.

Why we do it

Because the best business doesn’t always win the customer. The one who answers does.

A missed enquiry isn’t a missed chat. It’s an eleven thousand pound heat pump, a solar and battery install, a survey worth more than most firms take in a fortnight, quietly dialling the next number down the list. Skilled, trustworthy installers lose that work every week for one reason: they were up a ladder when it rang. That never sat right with us. Relance exists so the quality of your work, not the timing of a phone call, decides who gets the job.

Our promises

What you can hold us to

  • ✓We never invent prices or appointments. Every reply comes from your real business facts. Prices are given as ranges, firm figures need a site visit, and it never makes up a slot.
  • ✓It sounds like your business, not a call centre. Knowledgeable, local and to the point, the way you would text a customer back on a good day.
  • ✓It knows its limits. When a lead needs a human, it hands them to you rather than guessing, and flags it so nothing slips.
  • ✓We recover leads the right way. It replies to people who contacted you first, includes an opt-out, and is set up in line with UK PECR rules. No spam.
  • ✓No long tie-in. Cancel any time. We’d rather earn your place each month than trap you in a contract.
  • ✓You stay in control. When you pick up, nothing happens. Relance is a safety net, never a replacement for you.
