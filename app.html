What you should know

  • ✓It carries your name, not ours. The icon on your home screen, the name under it and the colours inside are your business. Your customers never see the word Relance.
  • ✓Nothing new to learn. It is set up for you during onboarding and arrives working. If you can use a text message, you can use this.
  • ✓Values are typical job values. The pounds you see are what that kind of job is normally worth for your business, taken from your own figures during setup, not invoices raised.
  • ✓You can always take over. Every conversation has a button that stops the system and hands it to you mid-sentence.
  • ✓Included in the monthly fee. The app and the dashboard come with Relance. There is nothing extra to buy.
