    The five questions 2027 must answer

    1Why do clients stay? First churn cohorts read. The ICP re-signed or corrected.
    2Does the month-two moment happen? Recovered revenue beating the subscription by day 60, measured not asserted.
    3Does the funnel convert? Visit to form to film to call to close, with two quarters of history.
    4Will anyone buy without you in the room? One client closed where you made no first contact.
    5Does attribution survive an argument? A sceptical client interrogates the number and agrees with it.

    Too late if left until 2027

    •Reference-quality service. The 2026 clients become the 2027 case studies become the 2028 channel.
    •Honest attribution. It can't be bolted on after fifty clients have seen a generous number.
    •Your time ledger. Start the audit now, at 100% ops, so the trend line exists.
