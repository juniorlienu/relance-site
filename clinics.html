The maths of a missed patient

One missed call isn't one missed session.

Patients are worth a course, not a session

A new patient isn't one £50 to £80 appointment. It's an assessment, a treatment plan, and often six to twelve sessions. Losing the first call loses the whole course of care.

Pain doesn't wait politely

Someone with an acute back is ringing down a list, and they book the first practice that responds. Being the best clinician doesn't matter if a competitor's phone gets answered and yours doesn't.

You're booked solid, so you miss the most

The fuller your diary, the more calls you miss, and the more your growth quietly stalls. The busiest practices leak the most new patients. That's the trap Relance closes.

What it does for a practice

Reception-desk care, without the reception desk

  • ✓Answers what new patients actually ask. Session prices, what the first appointment involves, whether you treat their issue, parking and hours. From your real information, never invented.
