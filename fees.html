What people ask on the call.

Do my customers know it's AI?
Lance speaks as your business and never announces itself. If someone asks outright whether they're talking to a person, it doesn't lie, it hands them to you. In practice the question almost never comes up, because it reads like your office manager texting back.
What if I answer the call myself?
Then nothing happens. Lance only steps in when a call rings out or a message lands that nobody's picked up. Pick up the thread at any point and it steps back.
Does it quote prices?
No. In this trade a price doesn't exist until somebody's seen the roof or done the heat loss. It says exactly that and books the survey. Once your quote's out, it chases it, and the moment price comes up it brings you in.
What do I have to do?
About an hour across two calls and a screen share to connect your channels. We do the rest. After that, keep some survey slots open each week and read the Monday text.
How fast is it live?
Days, not weeks. Your published number never changes. We connect your line, your channels and your diary, test it on real messages with you, then switch it on.
Is there a contract?
No. No setup fee, cancel any time. We'd rather earn the month than lock you in.
