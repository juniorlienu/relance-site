Straight answers

The questions every installer asks

Does this replace me answering my phone? +

Not at all — it's a safety net, not a replacement. When you pick up, nothing happens. It only steps in when a call would otherwise go to voicemail and be lost. You stay in full control; it just makes sure no one slips through when you can't get to the phone.

Do I need a new phone number? +

No. It's set up around the number you already use and advertise. Your customers call the same number they always have — the system works quietly behind it.

How fast can it be live? +

Typically within 48 hours. We load in your real prices, services, coverage area and hours so every reply is accurate to your business, then switch it on. There's nothing for you to install or learn.

Will it make up prices or promise things I can't deliver? +

No — and this is deliberate. It only ever answers from the genuine facts about your business. It quotes prices as ranges, always says a firm figure needs a site visit, and never invents an appointment slot. When it isn't sure, it hands the customer to you rather than guessing.

Am I tied into a contract? +

No long tie-in — you can cancel any time. We would rather keep you because the system is making you money than because a contract traps you. All we ask is a fair run so there is real data to judge it on.

Is automated texting allowed under UK rules? +

Yes, when done properly. The system replies to people who contacted you first, includes an opt-out, and is set up in line with UK PECR guidance — so you're recovering leads the right way, not spamming.
