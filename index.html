Good evening, I was just wondering if it's possible to install a battery even after my solar panels have been installed?

Thanks for getting in touch with us. Yes, we can add battery storage to your existing solar system. Typically runs £3,500 to £6,000 depending on the capacity you need, and we'd confirm the exact price at a site visit. What sort of electricity bills are you seeing at the moment?

Right now my usage is around £1,700 per annum.

That's helpful to know. Do you own the property, and is there any particular timescale you're working to?

I'm the property owner, and I'm set on getting the batteries installed as soon as possible.

Would you be looking to spread the cost, or pay outright? And what's your postcode so I can get you booked in for a survey?

I'm fine with paying outright. My address is UB5 6QJ.

Perfect. I've got a couple of survey slots available: Wednesday at 10am, or Thursday at 2pm. Which works better for you?

Wednesday at 10am is perfect.

That's booked in for Wednesday at 10am. Just make sure someone's home for the 90 minute visit, and if you have any paperwork from your original solar installation that would be useful.
