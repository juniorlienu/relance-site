Meet Lance

The member of staff who never misses a call.

Lance is the AI inside Relance. He answers the enquiries you cannot get to, in your voice, using your real prices. Your customers never learn his name, because as far as they are concerned they are talking to your business. You are the only one who sees him working.

The difference

Most AI is trained on the internet. Lance is trained on your business.

A generic chatbot knows what a boiler is. It does not know what you charge to fit one, which brands you will not touch, how far you travel, or that Tuesday mornings are kept for surveys. That is the difference between a reply that wins the job and a reply that loses it.

Before Lance answers a single message, we sit down and take your business apart: your prices, your services, your patch, your hours, and crucially your highest paying jobs. All of it goes into his head. Then we tune the way he writes until it sounds like you on a good day.

How he thinks

Four things Lance does that a chatbot does not

He knows what a job is worth

During setup we build a revenue map of your work. So when someone mentions a boiler that keeps breaking, he asks how old it is, because that enquiry might be a £2,600 replacement rather than a £90 repair. He qualifies upward, honestly, without ever turning down the small jobs.

He remembers the conversation

Ask a follow up and he answers it in context, without repeating the greeting or asking your customer to start again. It reads as one continuous conversation with a person, because that is what a real enquiry is.

He knows where his job ends

Emergencies, complaints, aftercare worries and anything he cannot answer from your facts go straight to you, flagged. He would rather fetch you than guess. That single rule is what makes him safe to put in front of your customers.

He books, he does not just chat

He offers real slots from your own diary and puts the appointment in. He will never invent a time to sound helpful. A booking is the outcome. A callback is admin.

The rules he cannot break

What Lance will never do

Trust is the whole product. These are hard limits, written into how he works, not preferences we hope he follows.

  • ✓He never invents a price. Every figure comes from your own numbers. Ranges are given as ranges, and anything that needs a proper look gets a site visit rather than a guess.
  • ✓He never invents an appointment. If your diary has no slot, he does not offer one.
  • ✓He never gives clinical or safety advice. In a clinic or an aesthetics practice, that is a human's job, and he hands it over immediately.
  • ✓He never pretends to be a robot, or a person. He speaks as your business, plainly. Every message he sends is signed in your app, so you always know which words were his.
  • ✓He never pesters. One nudge if someone goes quiet, then he stops. No drip campaigns aimed at your customers.
  • ✓He never overrides you. Pick up the phone or open the chat and he steps back. You can take over mid sentence.
