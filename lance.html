Meet Lance

The member of staff who never misses a call.

Lance is the AI inside Relance. It answers the enquiries you can't get to, in your voice, and gets them qualified and booked in. Your customers never learn the name, because as far as they're concerned they're talking to your business. You're the only one who sees Lance working.

The difference

Most AI is trained on the internet. Lance is trained on your business.

A generic chatbot knows what a heat pump is. It doesn't know what brands you don't work with, how far out you'll travel, what counts as an emergency for you, and so on. That's the difference between a response that wins the job and a response that loses it.

Before Lance answers a single message, we sit down and take your business apart: your services, your patch, your hours, what counts as an emergency, and crucially your highest paying jobs. All of it goes in. Then we tune the way Lance writes until it sounds like you on a good day.

How it works

Four things Lance does that a chatbot doesn't

Lance knows what a job is worth

During setup we build a revenue map of your work, so Lance knows what each kind of job is worth to you. That's how it spots the bigger job hiding inside a small enquiry. When someone asks about a repair, it asks how old the system is. When someone with panels mentions rising bills, it asks about storage. It qualifies upward, honestly, and never invents a bigger job than the one in front of it.

Lance remembers the conversation

Ask a follow up and it answers it in context, without repeating the greeting or asking your customer to start again. It reads as one continuous conversation with a person, because that's what a real enquiry is.

Lance knows where the job ends

Emergencies, complaints, aftercare worries and anything it can't answer from your facts go straight to you, flagged. It would rather fetch you than guess. That single rule is what makes it safe to put in front of your customers.

Lance books, doesn't just chat

It offers real slots from your own diary and puts the appointment in. It will never invent a time to sound helpful. A booking is the outcome. A callback is admin.

The rules it can't break

What Lance will never do

Trust is the whole product. These are hard limits, written into how Lance works, not preferences we hope it follows.

  • ✓Lance never puts a price on a job nobody has seen. Every install needs a survey and it says so, then moves the conversation to getting one booked. The only figures it ever gives are the ones you set yourself, such as a call-out charge or a survey fee.
  • ✓Lance never invents an appointment. If your diary has no slot, it doesn't offer one.
  • ✓Lance never gives advice it isn't qualified to give. Anything technical, clinical or safety related is a human's job, and it hands over immediately.
  • ✓Lance never pretends to be a person. It speaks as your business, plainly. Every message it sends is marked in your inbox, so you always know which words were its.
  • ✓Lance never pesters. One nudge if someone goes quiet, then it stops. No drip campaigns aimed at your customers.
  • ✓Lance never overrides you. Pick up the phone or open the chat and it steps back. You can take over mid sentence.
