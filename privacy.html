Privacy

Plain English, because you deserve it.

This explains what we do with the details you give us, why we are allowed to, and how to make us stop. Last updated 18 August 2026.

Who we are

Relance

Relance is a UK business providing missed enquiry recovery for service businesses. If you want to reach us about anything on this page, email hello@relance.co.uk and we will answer.

What we collect

Only what you hand us

If you fill in the form to watch a demo film, we collect your name, business name, email address and mobile number, along with the industry you selected and the date you watched. If you book a call, we collect what you put in the booking form. We use standard website analytics that count visits without identifying you personally.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Because you told us we could

The demo form asks for your consent, and we only store your details if you tick the box. That consent is our lawful basis for emailing and texting you about Relance. You can withdraw it whenever you like, and withdrawing it's as easy as giving it.

What we do with it

We talk to you about Relance. Nothing else.

We may email or text you about what Relance does, how it applies to your trade, and offers relating to it. We don't sell your details, we don't rent them, and we don't pass them to anyone outside the tools we use to run the business. Those tools are our customer system (GoHighLevel), our hosting (Vercel) and our email and text delivery, all of which act on our instructions only.

Making us stop

One word is enough

Reply STOP to any text and the texts end immediately. Click unsubscribe in any email and the emails end immediately. If you 'd rather we deleted everything we hold about you, email hello@relance.co.uk and we will delete it and confirm that we have. You're also entitled to ask for a copy of what we hold, and to have anything wrong corrected.

How long we keep it

Two years, unless you ask sooner

If you never become a client, we delete your details two years after your last contact with us. If you do become a client, we keep what we need for as long as we work together, and for six years afterwards where tax and accounting rules require it.

If we get it wrong

You can complain, and you should

Tell us first and we will try to put it right. If you're still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, which is the UK regulator for this.
